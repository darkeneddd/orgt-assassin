## Goal
Assassinate your target by clipping a provided carabiner on them. When you eliminate someone, you receive their target. Eliminate as many as you can.

## General Rules
- No breaking and entering.
- No disrupting tests or lectures.
- Use only game-provided weapons.
- No excessive physical contact. Do not hurt anyone.
- **Weapon pickup:** Oct 26 to Oct 30 at ORGT during open Outpost hours. Only grab one.

## Safe Zones
You cannot be assassinated in these places:
- ORGT classroom
- ORGT cages (inside only)
- Sport-wide meetings
- Classroom sessions for IP participants, and IP trips (including gear cleaning after)
- Inside an ORGT van or SUV
- Quiet places on campus (e.g. Crossland 6 and 7)
- In class (before and after class are fair game)
- At work (including labs)
Everything else is fair game, including GT campus, the ORGT parking lot, and home park.

## How to Assassinate
- Clip the carabiner for real. Placing it on someone doesn't count. A backpack they are carrying counts.
- Take a photo or video with your target and the weapon in frame.
- Submit it through the **Report a Kill** form with your name, your target's name, and the location.
- Make sure the eliminated person forwards you their target.

## How to Win
The game ends when one assassin remains. If no one remains alone when the game ends (Dec 2 at 11:59 pm), the assassin with the most kills wins.

Kills must be submitted through the Report a Kill form before the game ends. Reports after that do not count.

## Prize
The winner is crowned at ORGT Prom.

## Clarifications
- You may not steal or break others' carabiners. You may grab and toss one away if someone is attacking you.
- Lost carabiner? Get another at ORGT.
- Personal trips and training trips are not IP trips and are fair game.
- No assassinating someone who is driving.
- No clocking in or out of work just to assassinate.
- The organizer has the final call.

## FAQ
- **I lost my carabiner.** Get another at ORGT.
- **We disagree about a kill.** Contact the organizer. They have the final call.
- **Who has the final call?** The organizer.
- **What if I'm eliminated?** Forward your target to your killer.
